Privacy

The short version: we collect your email so we can tell you when Zaflick launches. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, or send you anything you did not sign up for.

What we collect

When you join the Circle (our early list), we collect the email address you submit. We also receive analytics about how the site is used: pages viewed, which pieces you opened the join form from, whether you chose the women's or men's view, and taps on share and WhatsApp links.

We use Microsoft Clarity to see, in aggregate, how people scroll and tap on the site (heatmaps and session replays), so we can fix what is confusing. Clarity hides what you type, such as your email address. Microsoft processes this data under its own privacy statement.

If you arrive from a campaign or a friend's referral link, we record the campaign and referral tags in that link so we know where sign-ups came from.

If you apply to be a Founding Tester or write to us through the contact page, we collect what you type into that form, such as your name, email, city, Instagram handle and your answers. We use it only to reply to you and to run that programme.

Analytics and advertising tools

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, and the Meta Pixel to measure sign-ups from Instagram and Facebook and to show Zaflick to people who have visited the site. These tools use cookies and similar technology, and Google and Meta process that data under their own privacy policies. You can block or clear these cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site works without them.

Why we collect it

Only to contact you about the launch: the drop date, early access and member benefits.

Where it is stored

Your email is stored securely in our database and mirrored into a private spreadsheet that only we can access. Data is sent over HTTPS.

Your browser also keeps a copy of your email, your member number and your women's or men's preference on your own device, so the site remembers you are already on the list. Clearing your browser data for this site removes that local copy. It does not remove you from the Circle — email admin@zaflick.com to be taken off.

What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your data
  • We never share it with advertisers or data brokers
  • We never send you spam, only the launch updates you signed up for

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time, including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the GDPR and CCPA where they apply. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe option, and you can reach us to opt out or request deletion at admin@zaflick.com.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update it here. Material changes to how we use your data will be communicated to members directly.

Contact

Questions about your data? Email admin@zaflick.com.
