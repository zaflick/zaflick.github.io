About Zaflick

Dressed for every good time with friends.

Zaflick is an Indian athleisure brand for the plans you make with friends: a walk, a game, a coffee, a day out. One outfit that is ready for any of them, and looks good the whole way through.

Why Zaflick

You know when friends make a plan and you think, I'm not dressed for that? We make clothes so that never happens. One outfit takes you from a walk to a game to dinner, and you look good the whole way.

Jeans can't do the game. Gym wear looks wrong at the coffee after. Zaflick does both. Five pieces for women and men, starting in Hyderabad and Bengaluru.

The founders

Two computer science grads walk into a clothing brand.

We're Rahul Raja Dunna and Hemanth Kumar Jami. Same city (Visakhapatnam), same degree, wildly different exits. Zaflick started in 2025, after one plan too many where one of us turned up dressed for the wrong half: gym clothes at dinner, jeans at the game. We figured we couldn't be the only ones. Before designing a single stitch, we asked around. The numbers are just below.

Rahul Raja Dunna

I'm Rahul, from Visakhapatnam, and the first person in my family to go into business. No pressure. I studied computer science, did an MBA at IIM Amritsar, and built a background in data science, with detours through sales, political strategy and product development. Marketing and sales are the fun part for me, and if there's a new AI tool out, I've probably tried it: I use them every day to work better.

Hemanth Kumar Jami

I'm Hemanth, also from Visakhapatnam: a traveller and a creative professional. After graduating in computer science engineering, I followed my passion and built a background in photography, capturing weddings and wildlife (both involve a lot of waiting for the perfect moment). In my free time, I'm out exploring diverse cultures. I'm also the one who makes everyone stop for one more photo.
