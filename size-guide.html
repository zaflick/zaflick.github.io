Size guide

Sizes are still being finalised. Sizing is built on Indian measurements, not on a chart borrowed from another market.

Is there a size chart yet?

Not yet. The chart is published with the real samples. Until then, the size guide explains how to measure yourself so you are ready when it goes up.

How to measure yourself

Use a soft tape over thin clothing. Keep it level and snug, not tight.

  1. 01

    Bust or chest

    Around the fullest part, under the arms, with the tape level across your back.

  2. 02

    Underbust

    Straight around, just below the bust. The number for sports bras.

  3. 03

    Waist

    Around the narrowest part, usually just above the belly button.

  4. 04

    Hips

    Around the fullest part of the hips and seat, with your feet together.

  5. 05

    Inseam

    From the top of the inner thigh to where you want the hem, standing straight.

Which measurement matters

PieceChoose byGood to know
Active leggingWaist, then hipsIf your hip measurement is the larger size, go by the hip.
Sports braUnderbustThe underband does most of the supporting.
Fitted jacketBust and waistCut to the natural waist.
Active T-shirtChestA relaxed, boxy fit by design.
Wide-leg pantsHips and inseamUnisex. The drawcord waist adjusts.
