Weekend plans · Hyderabad

Weekend plans with friends in Hyderabad

A walk, a game, a fort at sunset, chai in the Old City. Six plans for this weekend, and one outfit for all of them.

What to wear for all of them

One outfit you can walk, play and sit down to eat in: a top or sports bra, a light fitted jacket, wide-leg pants or a high-waisted legging with a phone pocket, and clean sneakers. From November to February, Hyderabad mornings are cool and afternoons warm and dry, so a light layer does most of the work.

  1. 01

    A morning walk at KBR Park

    KBR National Park, Jubilee Hills

    A track runs around the edge of the park, under the trees. Walk a lap or two, talk the whole way, and go for breakfast after.

    Her
    Sports bra, active legging and fitted jacket
    Him
    Active T-shirt and wide-leg pants

    Go early. It gets busy and warm by mid-morning at weekends.

  2. 02

    A game, then a long breakfast

    A badminton, pickleball or padel court near you

    Book a court for an hour, play doubles, then take the conversation to breakfast in Jubilee Hills or Banjara Hills.

    Her
    Sports bra, active legging and fitted jacket
    Him
    Active T-shirt and wide-leg pants

    Zip up the jacket when you sit down. Court sweat and café air-conditioning don't mix.

  3. 03

    Sunset at Golconda Fort

    Golconda Fort, west Hyderabad

    Climb to the top in the late afternoon for the view over the city, then stay on for the evening light show.

    Her
    Sports bra, wide-leg pants and fitted jacket
    Him
    Active T-shirt and wide-leg pants

    There are a lot of steps. Wear shoes with grip and keep your phone in a pocket, not your hand.

  4. 04

    A food walk in the Old City

    Charminar and Laad Bazaar

    Start at Charminar, walk through Laad Bazaar, and stop for Irani chai and Osmania biscuits along the way.

    Her
    Sports bra, wide-leg pants and fitted jacket
    Him
    Active T-shirt and wide-leg pants

    Streets are crowded. Keep your hands free and your phone in a deep pocket.

  5. 05

    An evening by Durgam Cheruvu

    Durgam Cheruvu, near Madhapur

    Walk across the cable bridge after dark, when it's lit up, then find dinner nearby.

    Her
    Sports bra, active legging and fitted jacket
    Him
    Active T-shirt and wide-leg pants

    Evenings by the lake can be breezy. Bring the jacket.

  6. 06

    An afternoon at Shilparamam

    Shilparamam crafts village, Madhapur

    Wander the craft stalls and food counters, pick up something handmade, and stay for whatever's on that evening.

    Her
    Sports bra, wide-leg pants and fitted jacket
    Him
    Active T-shirt and wide-leg pants

    It's a lot of walking on open ground. Go after the midday heat.
